# Customer Support Runbook

## Refunds

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are
processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method.

Subscriptions cancelled mid-cycle are not pro-rated; access continues until
the end of the paid period.

## Escalation

Tier 1 agents escalate to Tier 2 when an issue involves data loss, billing
disputes above 500 USD, or a suspected security problem.

Any issue open longer than 48 hours is automatically escalated to a team lead.
