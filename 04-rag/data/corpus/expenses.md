# Expense Policy

## Submission Deadline

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the date of purchase. Claims
submitted after 30 days require director approval and may be rejected.

## Limits

Daily meal allowance is 40 USD for domestic travel and 65 USD for
international travel. Hotel bookings above 250 USD per night require prior
approval from your department head.

Receipts are mandatory for any expense above 25 USD.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll run, which closes on the
20th of each month.
